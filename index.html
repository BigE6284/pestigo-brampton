Cockroach

Cockroaches

Cockroaches hide in warm, tight spaces near food and water, so you often see the signs before you see them.

Signs to look for

  • Small dark droppings, like pepper or coffee grounds
  • Sightings at night in kitchens and bathrooms
  • A musty, oily smell in cupboards
  • Egg casings behind appliances or in cabinets

What you can do today

  • Wipe up crumbs and don’t leave dishes out overnight
  • Keep food in sealed containers
  • Fix dripping taps and dry wet areas
  • Note where you see them so we can target treatment

Mouse

Mice & rats

As temperatures drop, mice and rats look for shelter indoors. Rodents can squeeze through surprisingly small gaps.

Signs to look for

  • Droppings along walls, in drawers or the pantry
  • Scratching sounds in walls or ceilings at night
  • Gnaw marks on food packaging or wood
  • Shredded paper or fabric used for nests

What you can do today

  • Check for gaps around doors, windows, pipes and vents
  • Store food securely; keep the pantry tidy
  • Keep utility and storage areas clear of clutter
  • Book an inspection if you’ve noticed droppings or scratching

Spider

Spiders

Spiders follow their food. Where there are lots of webs, there are usually other insects nearby too.

Signs to look for

  • Webs building up in corners, windows and basements
  • Egg sacs tucked into quiet spots
  • Nests around eaves, decks and window frames outside
  • More spiders indoors in late summer and fall

What you can do today

  • Vacuum webs and clear storage clutter
  • Keep outdoor lights off when not needed (they draw insects)
  • Check window screens for tears
  • Trim plants back from the house

Centipede

Centipedes

Centipedes love damp, dark spaces. Seeing them often points to moisture and other insects in the area.

Signs to look for

  • Fast-moving, many-legged pests in basements or bathrooms
  • Sightings at night, especially near drains
  • Activity around floor drains and laundry areas
  • Damp spots, leaks or high humidity nearby

What you can do today

  • Run a dehumidifier in the basement
  • Fix leaks and dry out damp corners
  • Clear boxes and clutter off the floor
  • Seal cracks along baseboards and foundation walls
