Cockroach Control

What to know.

Cockroach treatment is the service Pestigo’s Google reviewers mention most. In Brampton, cockroaches often show up in kitchens and bathrooms, and in homes that share walls, such as townhouses, condos and apartments.

Store-bought sprays can kill the roaches you see but usually miss the ones breeding behind appliances, inside cabinets and in wall voids. A professional treatment goes after where they live, not just where they’re spotted.

Signs of cockroaches

  • DroppingsSmall dark specks, like pepper or coffee grounds, in drawers, cupboards and along edges.
  • Night-time sightingsRoaches scattering when you switch on the kitchen or bathroom light.
  • Egg casingsSmall brown capsules behind appliances, under sinks or inside cabinets.
  • A musty, oily smellA stale odour that builds up in cupboards as an infestation grows.

Why cockroaches get into Brampton homes

  • Food crumbs, pet food and dishes left out overnight
  • Leaky taps, wet sinks and humid bathrooms
  • Shared walls and plumbing in townhouses and apartments
  • Hitchhiking in on boxes, grocery bags and second-hand appliances

Prevention tips

  • Keep food in sealed containers and wipe counters nightly
  • Don’t leave dishes or pet food out overnight
  • Fix drips and dry out wet areas under sinks
  • Seal cracks around pipes and baseboards
  • Take out garbage and recycling regularly

Can I get rid of cockroaches myself?
Store-bought products can knock down the cockroaches you see, but they often miss the ones breeding out of sight. If you keep seeing them, a professional treatment that targets where they hide is the more reliable fix.
