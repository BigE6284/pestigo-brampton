## Quote form
4-step request: pest, details plus photo, preferred date and time, and contact preference (call, text or email). It ends on a confirmation screen with a reference number.
**It doesn't submit to a server yet.** It opens the customer's email app addressed to info@pestigo.ca with everything filled in. Photos can't be attached automatically, so the confirmation screen reminds the customer to attach theirs.
Next step: connect a backend (e.g. Supabase or Formspree) so requests and photos arrive directly, with email alerts and a dashboard.

## To do before launch
- **Domain:** when there's a real domain, change `SITE_URL` in the build script and rebuild. Then add a `CNAME` file.
- **Google Search Console:** add the site, paste the HTML-tag token into `GSC_TOKEN`, rebuild, verify, then submit `sitemap.xml`.
- **Google Analytics 4:** create a property and put the Measurement ID in `GA_ID`. Phone taps (`phone_call_click`), quote requests (`generate_lead`) and opened quote emails (`quote_email_opened`) are already tracked as events.
- **Real photos:** add technician, vehicle and job photos (with customer permission) and list them in `PHOTOS`. A gallery appears in the "Why Pestigo" section automatically.
- **Reviews:** the rating and count are set by hand (`RATING`, `REVIEW_COUNT`). Review months come from Google's "x months ago" labels and may be off by a month. Showing a live rating needs the Google Places API.
- **Facts to confirm with Pestigo:** licence or insurance details to show, whether inspections are free, typical treatment times, whether residents must leave for each treatment, and the exact 6-month guarantee terms.
